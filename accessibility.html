Accessibility

Last updated: October 29, 2026

  2. What we do

Everyone should be able to find a flavor and send an order request. We are committed to making jerkyfixx.com usable for as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. That means working on things like readable color contrast, keyboard access, visible focus, clear headings and labels, and text alternatives for images.

What we do

  • Pages use headings and landmarks so screen reader users can move around.
  • Links, buttons and form fields can be reached and used with a keyboard.
  • Keyboard focus is visible.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, the site respects that setting.

Known limitations

  • 3D animations. The home page uses 3D and animated visuals. They can be hard to follow for some people. If you turn on “reduce motion” in your device or browser settings, the site respects it.
  • Third-party content. Embedded items such as Google Maps are provided by other companies, and we cannot fully control their accessibility.

Need help or want to report a problem?

If something on the site is hard to use, tell us and we will fix it or help you another way. You can also reach us by phone or text.

  • Email: jerkyfixx@gmail.com
  • Call or text: 505-922-5683 or 505-364-0678

Please tell us the page, what you were trying to do, and the device or assistive technology you use if you can.
