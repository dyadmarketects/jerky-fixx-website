About Mango Habanero

Mango Habanero is the sweet heat of the menu. The first thing you taste is mango, bright and a little sticky, and then the habanero sneaks in and keeps building after you've finished the strip.

Like every bag we make, it starts with thin cut beef that our family seasons and dries in small batches in Albuquerque. It's a good pick for folks who want real heat but like something sweet riding along with it.
