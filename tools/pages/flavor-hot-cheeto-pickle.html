About Hot Cheeto Pickle

It sounds wild. It sells out anyway. Hot Cheeto Pickle puts dill pickle tang and red hot cheeto heat on the same strip of thin cut carne seca, and somehow it just works.

Every batch is seasoned and dried by our family in Albuquerque. The pickle keeps it bright and sour, the cheeto dust brings the heat, and the beef holds it all together. It's the flavor people dare their friends to try, then buy for themselves.
