About Hot Cheetos

This is the flavor that gets the most double takes at the booth. We pack red hot cheeto dust into every strip of thin cut carne seca, so you get that crunchy, cheesy, red hot flavor on real beef jerky.

It's made in small batches by our family in Albuquerque. Your fingers will be red and you won't care. Grab a large bag if you're sharing, because it won't last.
