About Garlic Parm

Mellow on the heat, loud on the flavor. Garlic Parm brings roasted garlic and nutty parmesan to thin cut beef, with no burn at all.

It's made in small batches by our family in Albuquerque, and it's a favorite with people who say they don't like spicy food. Heads up, it contains dairy.
