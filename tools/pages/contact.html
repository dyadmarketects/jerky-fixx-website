How ordering works

  1. Build your order. Pick flavors, sizes and quantities in the form above, from the jerky menu or in your cart. They all share one cart.
  2. Send it with your delivery address. Add your name, phone, email and address so the dealer knows where to drop your fixx.
  3. We text to confirm. We confirm your order by text and take payment by Cash App, Venmo, Apple Pay or cash.
  4. Door drop off. Your dealer drops the order at your door and sends a photo once it is delivered.
