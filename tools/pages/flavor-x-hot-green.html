About X-Hot Green

This is the one the fiends ask for by name. X-Hot Green takes our classic New Mexico green chile carne seca and stacks even more green chile on top, so the heat builds with every strip and sticks around.

It's thin cut beef, seasoned heavy and dried slow by our family in Albuquerque. Expect that earthy, roasted green chile flavor first, then a burn that doesn't quit. If you grew up putting green on everything, this is your bag.
