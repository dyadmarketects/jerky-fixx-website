About Takis

If you know the rolled chile lime snack, you already know what this tastes like. Our Takis flavor brings that sour, spicy, lime heavy punch to thin cut carne seca, and it disappears fast.

Our family seasons and dries every batch in Albuquerque. The thin cut is the secret here: it soaks up the chile lime seasoning so every bite is loud. Fair warning, people tend to buy two.
