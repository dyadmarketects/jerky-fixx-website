The fine print, so far

How the waitlist works

Is the club open?
Not yet. It's coming soon, and the more people sign up, the sooner it happens.
What do I get for joining the waitlist?
A heads up by email when the club opens. Signing up doesn't commit you to anything and there is nothing to pay.
What will members get?
The plan: jerky mailed on your schedule, member pricing, members only promos and flavors, samples and swag in the box, and a vote on the next new flavor. Final details come when the club opens.
Can I still order now?
Of course. Order any of the 25 flavors from the jerky menu and we drop it at your door in Albuquerque or Rio Rancho.
