## What is small batch, anyway?

It means a family cuts, seasons and dries the beef in small amounts, then bags it up and brings it to you. The same crew you see at the table is the crew that makes it. Our carne seca starts with thin cut beef, gets a heavy hand with the seasoning and then dries long and slow. That's the whole secret. No big factory, just my family and a lot of love.
