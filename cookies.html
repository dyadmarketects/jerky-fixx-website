Cookie Notice

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Here is what your browser stores when you use jerkyfixx.com, grouped by category, and how to change your choice.

Your choices

Change your cookie preferences any time.

1. Strictly necessary

These make the site work and are always on. They are stored in your browser’s localStorage on your device and are not used for tracking.

NameWhat it does
jf_consentRemembers your cookie choice so we do not keep asking.
jf_cartKeeps the items in your cart until you send an order request.

2. Analytics (optional)

No analytics run by default. We may add Google Analytics 4 later to see which pages people visit and improve the site. If we do, it is optional and will only load after you accept it. If you decline, it will not load.

3. Third-party content

To display the site, your browser loads files from outside services, which receive your IP address and basic browser details:

  • Google Fonts (typefaces)
  • cdnjs and jsDelivr (scripts)
  • Google Maps (embedded map)

These providers may set their own cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. If you follow links to our Instagram, Facebook or Threads pages, those platforms apply their own rules too.

How to manage storage

  • Use the Cookie preferences button above.
  • Clear this site’s data in your browser settings. This also empties your cart.
  • Use your browser’s privacy or tracking-protection settings.
