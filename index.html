Jerky questions

Got questions?

Where can I buy jerky in Albuquerque?

Find Jerky Fixx at pop ups, festivals like New Mexico Jerky Fest at Isleta Resort & Casino, and local ball games. Check the dealer locator, or text us to order and arrange pickup.

Do you sell beef jerky in Rio Rancho?

Yes. We show up at Rio Rancho and Albuquerque ball games and pop ups all season. Message us for the next stop or to order ahead.

What is your spiciest jerky?

X-Hot Green is our hottest green chile, and Hot Mamma Cheeto and X-Hot Green Chili Lime Salt bring serious heat too. Hot Cheetos, Takis and Mango Habanero bring snack style heat.

Do you have Hot Cheeto jerky?

We do. Hot Cheetos and Hot Cheeto Pickle are crowd favorites, along with Takis and Tajin Limon.

Is carne seca the same as jerky?

Both are dried, seasoned beef. Carne seca is the traditional New Mexico name. Ours is small batch and chile forward, with fun snack inspired flavors.

How much is a bag, and how do I pay?

Small bags are $7 and large bags are $20. Pay with Cash App, Venmo, Apple Pay or cash. Jerky makes a great New Mexico gift.
