New Mexico in a bag

What makes it New Mexico

Around here, green chile goes on everything, and jerky is no exception. We start with thin cut beef, season it heavy with green chile, and dry it slow until it's chewy, crisp at the edges and packed with flavor. That's the carne seca style folks in Albuquerque grew up on. Curious about the difference? Read what carne seca is.

Searching for Hatch chile jerky or green chili jerky? You're in the right place. Our green chile lineup is the most popular part of the menu, and our green chile jerky ranked by heat guide helps you pick your level.

  • Can't decide?Start with Green, the classic.
  • Want both?X-Mas is red and green together.
  • Fiend level?X-Hot Green, if you dare.
