The people behind the bags

Meet the Fixx Family

Jerky Fixx is owned by husband and wife Joseph Garduno and Sabrina Penn Garduno, and run with their kids. The same crew cuts, seasons and dries every batch, then posts up behind the table at pop ups, festivals and a whole lot of softball games around Albuquerque and Rio Rancho.

Their daughter Aaliyah Garduno manages and maintains this website and writes the Fixx Blog. For the longer version, read Meet the Jerky Fixx Family.
